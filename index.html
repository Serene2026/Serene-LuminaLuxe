About Serene

At Serene, we believe in creating products that bring peace and beauty into your everyday life. Whether celebrating a milestone, showing appreciation, or simply indulging yourself, Serene's candles transform any moment into something memorable and meaningful. Our journey began with a simple mission: to craft premium candles that transform any space into a sanctuary of calm.

We've expanded our offerings to include custom sublimation items, allowing you to personalize mugs, water bottles, tiles, and stone prints with your favorite designs, memories, and messages.

Every product is made with attention to detail, using high-quality materials and sustainable practices. From our hand-poured wax candles with natural essential oils. To our durable sublimation items, we're committed to excellence in every piece.

Thank you for choosing Serene. Let us help you create moments of tranquility and joy in your daily routine. The Perfect Gift for Every Occasion

Shipping Information

We are committed to delivering your orders promptly and efficiently. We ship islandwide across Sri Lanka using reliable courier partners to ensure your products arrive safely and on time.

Shipping Methods & Delivery Times

We offer standard shipping for all orders. Typically, orders are processed within 1-3 business days, with delivery times ranging from 5-10 business days depending on your location.

Shipping Costs

We use (Freight on Board) FOB method where the buyer should carryout the shipping costs for the delivery. These costs are calculated at checkout based on the weight and dimensions of your order, as well as your delivery location. We strive to keep our shipping rates competitive and affordable.

International Shipping

Currently, we only ship within Sri Lanka. We hope to expand our shipping options in the future to serve our international customers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about shipping, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help!

Frequently Asked Questions

@ Here are some of the most common questions we receive from our customers. If you have a question that isn't answered here, please feel free to contact us.

1. What products do you offer?

We offer handcrafted candles and custom sublimation items such as mugs, water bottles, tiles, rock plates, printing services and more.

2. How can I place an order?

You can browse our products on the Shop page and add items to your cart. Once you're ready, proceed to checkout to complete your purchase.

3. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept only online bank transfers. And you can find our bank details at checkout. After completing the transfer, please send us a screenshot of your confirmation to WhatsApp.

4. How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary based on your location and the shipping method selected. Typically, orders are processed within 1-3 business days, with delivery times ranging from 5-10 business days for standard shipping.

5. Do you ship islandwide?

Yes we do, We deliver across Sri Lanka through reliable courier partners.

6. Can I see a preview before printing?

Yes, we provide digital proofs for all custom sublimation items before printing. This allows you to review and approve the design to ensure it meets your expectations.

7. Are your candles scented?

Yes! Only selected candles come in calming fragrances, and scented versions are available on request.

8. How long do the candles burn?

Burn time varies depending on size and shape, usually between 2-100 hours.

9. Are sublimation prints permanent?

Yes, sublimation prints are long-lasting and fade-resistant when cared for properly.

10. What is your return policy?

We send a digital proof for approval before printing. Custom products are non-refundable. On that count, customers get the best product.
